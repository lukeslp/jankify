▲ Jankify — Privacy Policy

Last updated 12 November 2026

Jankify collects nothing. There is no account, no sign-up, and no analytics inside the app. Every photo, GIF or video you open is processed entirely on your device and never leaves it unless you share the result yourself.

What the app does with your photos and videos

When you choose a photo, a GIF or a video, Jankify reads it into memory, transforms it on-device, and shows you the result. Nothing is uploaded to any server. When you export or share, the file goes only where you send it through the standard system share sheet.

What stays on your device

Jankify does not keep the photos and videos you open. Two things are stored, both only on your device:

  • To hand an export to the share sheet, the app writes that file to its own temporary storage. On Android the next export replaces it.
  • The Create workspace remembers your most recent generated pieces (up to 24 settings and small thumbnails) so you can reopen them.

Jankify sends neither anywhere. Clearing the app's storage or removing the app deletes them. The Android app, in every version from 1.0.6 on, also keeps both out of Android's cloud backup and device-to-device transfer. On iPhone and iPad the list of recent pieces is ordinary app data, so it is included in your own device backup if you keep one.

Data collected

None. Jankify does not collect, store, or transmit personal data, usage data, identifiers, or diagnostics. It makes no network requests of its own. There are no ads and no third-party SDKs.

Permissions

Jankify reads a photo or video only at the moment you pick one, through the system picker: the photo picker on iPhone and iPad, the file picker on Android. It does not request your full photo library, your location, your camera, or any other permission. The Android app has no internet permission at all.

Children

Jankify collects no data at all, so it collects none from children. It has no ads, no purchases and no chat. The App Store rates it 4+. Each store sets its own age rating.
