▲ Jankify — Privacy Policy

Last updated 2 September 2026

Jankify collects nothing. There is no account, no sign-up, and no analytics inside the app. Every image you open is processed entirely on your device and never leaves it.

What the app does with your photos

When you choose a photo, Jankify reads it into memory, transforms it on-device, and shows you the result. Nothing is uploaded to any server. When you export or share, the file goes only where you send it through the standard system share sheet. Jankify keeps no copies.

Data collected

None. Jankify does not collect, store, or transmit personal data, usage data, identifiers, or diagnostics. It makes no network requests of its own. There are no ads and no third-party SDKs.

Permissions

Jankify asks for access to a photo only at the moment you pick one, through the standard system photo picker. It does not request your full photo library, your location, your camera roll metadata, or any other permission in the background.

Children

Jankify is rated for all ages and is safe for children. Because it collects no data at all, it collects no data from children.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, the updated version will be posted on this page.
